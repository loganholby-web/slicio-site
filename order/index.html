Slicio is not open yet, so there is nothing to order today and we are not taking pickup requests. Here is how we plan it to work.

The plan

Order ahead, grab and go.

These are plans, not promises, and they can change.

  • Pick from the board

    Choose this week’s giant slices and the two permanent ones on the menu.

  • Add sauces on the side

    Every side sauce can go in the same order, with what is in it and the allergens listed.

  • Send a pickup request

    You sign in with your Crust Club account and send the request. Nothing is charged on this site.

  • Pickup times are not set

    We have not set pickup times, prices or limits yet. We would rather say so than guess.
