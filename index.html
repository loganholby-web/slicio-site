Straight answers.

All questions

Are you open yet?

Not yet. Slicio is coming soon to the Chicago suburbs. We don’t have an address, hours or prices to share, and we won’t make any up. When they’re real, they’ll show up here.

Who is behind Slicio?

Three high school seniors and a junior in the Chicago suburbs: Logan, Christian, Nino and Connor. Slicio is the company they started in their Incubator class.

How does the weekly menu work?

Four giant slices rotate in every week, and two permanent slices, The OG Pepperoni and The OG Cheese, never leave the board. The new lineup is revealed on Sunday at noon (Chicago time) and runs Monday to Saturday. See the menu.

What is the Crust Club?

Our members program for ages 13 and up. Every slice you buy earns 1 crust. Six crusts earn a free slice. Buy a slice three weeks in a row and you earn a bonus crust. Crusts last 6 months, and buying a slice every month keeps your rank. A free slice can be used on any slice. If a slice is refunded, its crusts come off and it stops counting toward your rank. Members also vote on the Slice of the Month. How it works.

How does the Slice of the Month vote work?

Each Crust Club member gets one vote per month. The ballot lists slices from that month’s rotation and opens for the last 7 days of the month. The winner stays on the board for the whole next month. See this month’s ballot.

Can I order or pay online?

Not yet. Ordering and payment come at launch. Until then, nothing on this site is for sale.
