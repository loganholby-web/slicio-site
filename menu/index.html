Preview lineup, week 42

The board

Four giant slices rotate in every week. Two permanent slices never leave. Nothing is on sale yet, and this is a preview of how a Slicio week will look.

Theme: Pizza Month: Tavern

This week’s 4

  1. 01

    Tavern Cheese

    Cracker-thin crust, mozzarella to the edge, square cut

    Chicago tavern style, plain: paper-thin crisp crust, sauce and mozzarella out to the very edge, cut in squares.

    Vegetarian

    ContainsMilkWheat

    Goes with Marinara and Garlic Parm

  2. 02

    Tavern Sausage

    Cracker-thin crust, sausage, party cut

    Chicago tavern style: paper-thin crisp crust, sausage to the edge, cut in squares.

    ContainsMilkWheat

    Goes with Marinara and Hot Honey

  3. 03

    Hot Giardiniera Sausage

    Thin crust, sausage, hot giardiniera

    Chicago tavern thin with sausage and spicy pickled vegetables. Crackery, sharp and a little oily in a good way.

    ContainsMilkWheat

    Goes with Marinara and Ranch

  4. 04

    Slice of the Month

    Member vote

    Picked by Crust Club members. The winner stays on the board all month.

    Allergens depend on the winner

Always on the board

  1. A1

    The OG Pepperoni

    Cup-and-char pepperoni, whole-milk mozzarella

    Always on the board. Pepperoni cups curl and char at the edges and hold a little pool of spicy oil. Add Hot Honey if you feel brave.

    ContainsMilkWheat

    Goes with Hot Honey and Ranch

  2. A2

    The OG Cheese

    Red sauce and whole-milk mozzarella, nothing else

    Always on the board. Red sauce, a lot of whole-milk mozzarella, and nothing to hide behind. Everyone likes cheese. The one to hand a kid, or to judge us by.

    Vegetarian

    ContainsMilkWheat

    Goes with Marinara and Garlic Parm

Good to know

  • Allergens. Every slice and sauce lists the major allergens it contains. If you have a serious allergy, ask the team before you eat. Cross-contact is possible in any kitchen that handles wheat, milk and egg.
  • Drinks. We’re planning a grab-and-go fridge with cans and bottles. No soda fountain.
  • Seating. We’re planning mostly takeout, with a few stools.
  • Plans change. We’re not open yet. Everything here is a plan, and it can change before launch.
