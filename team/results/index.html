Dates are Chicago dates and go by the day a rating was first made, so a later change does not move it. With no taste test chosen, ratings of the menu slices are included. Ratings that look fake are not counted unless you tick the box. Taste test ratings are left out for a throwaway email address, a phone number another account has, a rating made within 10 seconds of making the account, or a burst of copied comments. Menu slice ratings are left out for the first three of those. Ratings from members the team left out are left out too.

The summary has one line per pizza. The every rating file has one line per rating with the source, device and comment, and no names.
