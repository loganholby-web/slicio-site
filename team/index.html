The team

Four founders. One oven.

Slicio is the company we started in our Mundelein High School Incubator class: three seniors and a junior. Here’s who’s who.

  • Logan Holby

    Founder & CEO

    Logan is a senior at Mundelein High School. He started his first company, the apparel brand LYFTED, in 8th grade, and he is the founder and CEO of Nexus Pen, an AI smart pen that won Pitch Night 2026. At Slicio he leads the team and the business plan.

    LinkedIn profile of Logan Holby (opens in a new tab)

  • Christian Saucedo

    COO

    Christian is a senior at Mundelein High School studying cybersecurity and IT, and he works part-time as a service advisor. At Slicio he handles operations, service standards and launch planning.

    LinkedIn profile of Christian Saucedo (opens in a new tab)

  • Antonino “Nino” Maniscalco

    CMO

    Nino is a senior at Mundelein High School. He loves to cook and plans to open his own restaurant one day. At Slicio he leads marketing and the brand, and our recipes are tested in his Ooni pizza oven.

    LinkedIn profile of Antonino “Nino” Maniscalco (opens in a new tab)

  • Connor Russell

    CFO

    Connor is a junior at Mundelein High School and also works for Wise Choice Remodeling & Painting. At Slicio he manages the finances: pricing, food costs and the budget.
