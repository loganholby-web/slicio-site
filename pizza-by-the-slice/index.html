What we’re building

Pizza by the slice, coming soon.

Slicio Pizza is a pizza-by-the-slice company coming to the Chicago suburbs: four giant slices that change every week, plus two that never leave the board. We are not open yet and nothing is on sale.

We are the team behind Slicio, so we wrote this page ourselves.

What “by the slice” means here

You order one big slice instead of a whole pie. It works for one person, a lunch, or a group where everyone wants something different.

How the weekly menu works

  • Four rotating slices. Every Sunday at 12 PM Chicago time we reveal four new slices. They are on the board Monday to Saturday.
  • Two permanent slices. The OG Pepperoni and The OG Cheese stay on the board.
  • Slice of the Month. In the last week of each month, Crust Club members vote on slices from that month’s rotation, and the winner stays on the board the next month.

See the menu for a preview of how a Slicio week will look. Nothing on it is on sale yet.

The members program: Crust Club

Ages 13 and up. Crust Club is not live yet.

  1. Buy a slice, earn 1 crust.
  2. Reach 6 crusts and your next slice is free.
  3. Buy a slice 3 weeks in a row and earn a bonus crust.

Crusts last 6 months. A month with no slice drops your rank one step. There are four ranks, from Dough to Inner Crust. Read the Crust Club page and the terms.

Who we are

Slicio is a student company run by a team of high school students in Illinois. We have been filming test bakes while we get ready to open. Meet the team, or go to the Slicio home page to see this week’s slices.

What is not ready

  • We are not open and have no shop address yet.
  • Crust Club and member voting are not live yet.

FAQ

Can I order a slice?

Not yet. Nothing is on sale. Watch sliciopizza.com for the opening news.

Where will it be?

In the Chicago suburbs. We have not announced a location.

How do I earn a free slice?

Through Crust Club: 6 crusts earn a free slice. It is for ages 13 and up and is not live yet.

Are there allergens?

Every slice on the menu lists its allergens, such as milk, wheat and tree nuts. Check the menu page before ordering.
