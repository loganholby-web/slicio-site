Crust Club terms

These are the rules of the Crust Club, Slicio’s rewards program. The Crust Club isn’t live yet, and these rules apply from the day it starts.

Who can join

You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, we suggest checking with a parent or guardian first. We ask for a first name and an email address, and a password if you make an account with an email. A last name, a phone number, a birthday month and day, and a nearest store are optional.

How crusts work

  • Every slice you buy earns 1 crust. Free slices don’t earn crusts.
  • Buy at least one slice in 3 weeks in a row and you earn 1 bonus crust.
  • Rating a pizza at a Slicio taste test earns 1 crust, once per pizza.
  • 6 crusts earn 1 free slice.

The app and site keep your crust count. A free slice can be used on any slice.

Crusts expire

  • A crust can be used through the day 6 months after the day you earned it, on the Chicago calendar. A crust earned on March 15 can be used through September 15. If that day doesn’t exist in the month, the last day of the month counts: a crust earned on August 31 can be used through the last day of February.
  • When you claim a free slice, your oldest crusts are used first.
  • A crust that has expired doesn’t count toward a free slice and can’t be brought back.
  • Every crust expires the same way: crusts for slices, bonus crusts, the free slice credited when you reach Cheese (it comes as 6 crusts) and crusts from taste tests.
  • The app shows when your crusts expire.

Ranks

  • Your rank comes from the paid slices you buy in the current calendar year. The calendar year runs from January 1 to December 31, Chicago time (America/Chicago).
  • Only paid slices count. Free slices don’t count toward a rank, and a refunded slice stops counting (see Refunds).
  • Everyone starts at Dough. You reach Sauce at 12 paid slices in a year, Cheese at 40, and Inner Crust at 100.
  • You keep the highest rank you reach in a year through December 31 of the next year, as long as you buy a paid slice every month (see the monthly streak below). On January 1 the count starts again at 0.
  • A higher crust rate, such as double crusts at Inner Crust, starts with the purchases you make after you reach the rank, not before.
  • Every rank gets the same vote: 1 vote per member per month for the Slice of the Month. A higher rank never means more votes.

Monthly streak

  • Buy at least one paid slice in every calendar month, Chicago time, to keep your rank.
  • If a month ends with no paid slice, your rank drops one rank on the 1st of the next month. It never drops below Dough. If you skip two months in a row, it drops two ranks, one on each 1st.
  • A month with a paid slice climbs your rank back up one rank on the 1st of the next month, up to the rank your paid slices this year earned (or the rank you are keeping from last year).
  • Reaching the number of paid slices for a rank still takes you to that rank right away.
  • The app shows what you need to do this month and what happens on the 1st.

The Top 20

  • Slicio keeps a private Top 20 of the members with the most paid slices in the current calendar year, Chicago time.
  • You see only your own spot, in the app: your place if you are in the Top 20, or how many paid slices you are from it. No one else sees your place, and members never see names, counts or the list.
  • Ties share a place. If members tie for 20th place, they are all in the Top 20.
  • Only paid slices count, and a refunded slice stops counting. Team accounts and accounts we have left out of the results aren’t counted.
  • The count starts again at 0 on January 1.

Rank perks

  • Sauce: 1 free side sauce with every order.
  • Cheese: pre-ordering the new lineup on Sunday, right after the reveal, once ordering exists. Also 1 free slice, credited the day you reach Cheese, once per calendar year.
  • Inner Crust: double crusts (2 per paid slice). An invitation to the monthly tasting night, when we have a kitchen to host it. Once a year, a chance to pitch a slice: the team may pick one Inner Crust member’s slice for the board for one week. A yearly Inner Crust pin, when pins are available.

Tasting nights and pins start only when we can offer them. We can change, add or remove perks, and we can change the numbers above. We’ll post any change on this page before it applies.

Birthday slice

This is planned for when Slicio opens. It isn’t live yet, and nothing can be claimed today. We’ll say on this page when it starts.

  • One free slice a year, on your birthday. It is good for 7 days: your birthday and the 6 days after it, Chicago time.
  • You qualify only if your account and your birthday were both saved at least 3 months before that birthday. We count 3 months as 90 days.
  • If you change your birthday, the 90 days start over from the day you change it. That is so a free slice can’t be had by making an account or typing in a birthday just before it.
  • If your birthday is February 29, we celebrate it on February 28 in years that have no February 29.
  • You add your birthday as a month and a day, never a year. It is optional.
  • The birthday slice doesn’t change your crusts, your rank or your votes. It has no cash value.

No cash value

Crusts, free slices, ranks and perks have no cash value. They can’t be sold, traded, transferred or turned into cash.

Voting rules

  • Each member gets 1 vote per month for the Slice of the Month.
  • The ballot lists slices from that month’s rotation.
  • The ballot opens for the last 7 days of the month.
  • The winner is on the board for the whole next month.
  • We may remove votes that look duplicated or fake.

Refunds

If a slice is refunded, the crusts you earned on that slice are taken off your balance, and the refunded slice no longer counts toward your rank. It counts as one less paid slice, in the month and year you bought it. Your balance can’t go below 0.

Changes

We can change or end the Crust Club. If we change the rules or end the Crust Club, we’ll tell you in the app and on this website at least 30 days before it happens, so you have time to use your crusts. We’ll post the changes on this page. See also our privacy page.
