Who has the problem: students (2,127 at Mundelein High School, 2,100+), families (11,632 households, 11,000+), teams, clubs and offices, and early followers who like a new drop every week.

Empathy map "no food variety" (Ch 1.3), full text. Think and feel: same meals get boring, they want new flavors. Hear: friends suggest new places to eat, weekly specials. See: the same food options nearby, new foods on TikTok and Instagram. Say and do: "I want something different", they ask friends. Pains: bored with the same meals, new options cost too much, unsure they will like new food. Gains: meals they enjoy, affordable new flavors, new choices every week.

Competition, what the research says

Three facts we found.

giant slices in Mundelein or nearby towns

Crumbl sales per store, 2022 to 2025

Google like a pro: no shop in Mundelein, Libertyville, Vernon Hills, Grayslake or Lake Zurich sells a giant slice; the closest are in Chicago and Geneva at $6.00 to $8.50.

Online menu price scan: 223 menus read, 46 list a cheese slice, median $5.05 (Chicago $5.00, suburbs about $5.90). Our $6.50 giant slice is about 18% cheaper per square inch than a typical $5.00 slice.

Crumbl study: average store sales fell from about $1.8M (2022) to about $1.1M (2025), so a new menu every week is not enough by itself.
