The rules

  1. Members only. One vote per Crust Club member, per month.
  2. Last 7 days. The ballot opens for the last 7 days of the month.
  3. Winner stays. It’s on the board for the whole next month.
  4. No counts yet. We won’t show vote totals until voting is real.
