Vote for the Slice of the Month

Crust Club members get one vote a month. The ballot has four slices from that month’s rotation. The winner stays on the board for the whole next month.

This month’s four candidates

  1. 01

    Italian Beef

    Thin sliced beef, sweet pepper, provolone, hot giardiniera

  2. 02

    Tavern Sausage

    Cracker-thin crust, sausage, party cut

  3. 03

    Deep Dish Spinach

    Buttery crust, mozzarella, spinach, tomato

  4. 04

    Fennel Sausage

    Pinched sausage with fennel and a little chili

Voting opens at launch

No votes are being counted yet.

The Crust Club isn’t live, so there’s no ballot to cast and no vote count to show. The waitlist opens at launch too, so check back soon.

The rules

  1. Members only. One vote per Crust Club member, per month.
  2. Last 7 days. The ballot opens for the last 7 days of the month.
  3. Winner stays. It’s on the board for the whole next month.
  4. No counts yet. We won’t show vote totals until voting is real.
