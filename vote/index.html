The November vote

November 24 to 30

The ballot opens for the last 7 days of the month. Every Crust Club member gets one vote per month. The winner is the Slice of the Month next month.

Voting is in the Slicio app for iPhone, for Crust Club members. Crust Club is free for ages 13 and up. Get the app

The rules

  1. Members only. One vote per Crust Club member, per month.
  2. Last 7 days. The ballot opens for the last 7 days of the month.
  3. Winner stays. It’s on the board for the whole next month.
  4. Winner only here. Members who vote see the count in the app. This page shows the winner once voting closes.
