Crust Club

Six crusts. One free slice.

Every slice you buy earns a crust. Collect six and the next slice is on us. The Crust Club isn’t live yet, and this page shows how it will work.

3 of 6 crusts

Three more and the next slice is on us.

A demo of how it works. Nothing is tracked or saved.

How it works

Four rules. That’s it.

  1. 1

    Every slice earns a crust.

    Buy a slice, get 1 crust. Each crust drops one pepperoni into your slice.

  2. 2

    Six crusts is a free slice.

    Fill all six spots and your next slice is on us.

  3. 3

    Keep a streak, earn a bonus.

    Buy at least one slice in 3 weeks in a row and you earn 1 bonus crust.

  4. 4

    One vote a month.

    Members vote for the Slice of the Month. The winner stays on the board all month.

Ranks

Four ranks. Build the slice.

The more slices you buy in a year, the higher your rank. Each rank adds a layer to your badge and a perk to your Crust Club.

  1. Dough

    Join. Ages 13 and up.

    From day one

    • 1 crust for every paid slice
    • 6 crusts is a free slice
    • A bonus crust for a 3-week streak
    • 1 Slice of the Month vote a month

  2. Sauce

    12 paid slices a year

    About one a month.

    Everything in Dough, plus

    • 1 free side sauce with every order

  3. Cheese

    40 paid slices a year

    Everything in Sauce, plus

    • Pre-order the new lineup on Sunday night, right after the reveal (once ordering exists)
    • 1 free slice the day you reach Cheese (once per calendar year)

  4. Inner Crust

    The hard one

    100 paid slices a year

    About 2 a week.

    Everything in Cheese, plus

    • Double crusts: 2 per paid slice, so a free slice every 3
    • An invite to the monthly tasting night, to try next month’s ballot slices first (when we have a kitchen)
    • Once a year, pitch a slice. The team picks one Inner Crust member’s slice for the board for one week
    • A yearly Inner Crust pin
  • Paid slices only. Free slices don’t count toward a rank.
  • One calendar year. The count runs from January 1 to December 31, Chicago time, and starts again at 0 on January 1.
  • Keep it a while. Reach a rank and it is yours through December 31 of the next year.
  • Starts after you rank up. A higher crust rate counts from the purchases you make after you reach the rank.
  • Same vote for everyone. 1 vote per member per month at every rank.

Ranks are a plan, and the Crust Club isn’t live yet. The numbers and perks can change before launch. Read the draft terms.

The fine print, in plain words

  • Ages 13 and up. We ask for a first name and an email. A last name, a phone number, a birthday month and day, and a nearest store are optional. We never ask for a birth year.
  • No cash value. Crusts and free slices can’t be sold, traded or turned into cash.
  • Not live yet. There’s nothing to sign up for today, and the demo above doesn’t save anything.
  • Draft terms. Read the Crust Club draft terms.
