Catering

Slices for the whole room.

Teams, clubs, classrooms and parties. Catering is part of our launch plan. Send us a request and we’ll write back about what we can do.

The plan

Here’s what we’re working on.

These are plans, not promises, and they can change.

  • Slices by the box

    Pick slices from the week’s lineup and we box them up for the group.

  • Pickup first

    We’re planning pre-ordered pickups before anything else.

  • Sauces on the side

    Add dips and drizzles from the side sauce list.

  • No numbers yet

    Prices, minimums and lead times aren’t set. We’d rather say so than guess.
