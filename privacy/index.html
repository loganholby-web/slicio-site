Draft, updated October 11, 2026

Privacy

This is a plain-language draft written by students. It will be reviewed before the site opens, and we’ll update it when anything changes.

The short version

We collect a name and an email address, only if you type them into a form on this site. We don’t ask for birthdays. We don’t run ads, trackers or analytics on this site.

What we collect, and when

  • Waitlist. Your name and email, so we can tell you when Slicio opens.
  • Catering inquiry. Your name, email and what you write about your event.
  • Slice of the Month vote. The email you vote with and the slice you pick, so each member counts once a month.

These forms are not open yet. Until they open, this site collects nothing from you.

What we don’t collect

No birthdays, home addresses, phone numbers or payment details. We don’t have accounts yet. This site doesn’t set cookies, and it loads its fonts and code from its own files, not from other companies.

How we use it

To tell you when we open, to answer a catering inquiry, and to count votes. We won’t sell your information.

Who can see it

The Slicio team, and the service that stores form submissions. We’ll name that service here before any form opens. The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, the way most hosts do.

Ages

The Slicio Crust Club and waitlist are for ages 13 and up. If you are under 13, please don’t send us anything. If we learn that we collected information from someone under 13, we’ll delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete what you gave us. We don’t have a public email address yet, so we’ll add the way to reach us on this page before any form opens.
