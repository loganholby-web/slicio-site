Privacy

This is a plain-language draft written by students. It will be reviewed before Slicio opens, and we’ll update it when anything changes.

The short version

If you make a Slicio account, we keep your first name and email, and the things you do with the account: crusts, votes and taste test ratings. We don’t ask for birthdays. We don’t run ads, trackers or analytics on this site. We won’t sell your information.

What we store

When you sign in to the Slicio app, or to the taste test page on this site, we store:

  • Your first name and email. They come from Apple or Google when you sign in. If you use Apple’s Hide My Email, we get Apple’s private relay address instead of your real one.
  • Your member code. A short code we make for you, so the team can find your Crust Club at the counter.
  • Your crusts. Each crust or free slice added to or spent from your account, with the date.
  • Your taste test ratings. The rating you give a test slice, whether you would buy it, and the comment you write, if any.
  • Your Slice of the Month votes. The slice you pick each month, so each member counts once a month.
  • What you send from a form in the app. A pickup request, a catering inquiry or a waitlist signup, with what you typed in it.

Apple or Google may send other details when you sign in, such as a profile photo address. Our sign-in service may keep them in its own record. We don’t read them, show them or use them, and deleting your account removes that record.

On the site, the waitlist, catering and vote forms are not open yet. Until they open, those forms collect nothing from you.

How you sign in

You sign in with Apple or Google, or with an email code in the app. Slicio never sees a password. The database and the sign-in service are run for us by Supabase, which hosts them. Apple and Google follow their own privacy rules for the sign-in step.

The taste test page

The taste test page (the one a pizza card’s QR code opens) runs in your browser. It asks our database for the pizza’s name and, if you rate the slice, saves your rating there. When you tap Continue with Google, it sends you to Google to sign in and brings you back. To keep you signed in, it saves your sign-in in your browser’s local storage on your own device. It also saves your answer to “I am 13 or older” there, and that answer is never sent to us. It doesn’t set cookies. Sign out clears the sign-in. Clear this site’s data in your browser settings to clear the age answer.

What we don’t collect

No birthdays, home addresses, phone numbers or payment details. This site loads its fonts and code from its own files, not from other companies. The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, the way most hosts do.

How we use it

To run the Crust Club, count votes, learn which test slices people liked, answer catering inquiries, and tell you when we open.

Who can see it

The Slicio team can see your account and what you did with it. Your taste test comments are seen only by the Slicio team. The results screens show each pizza’s average rating and comments without names, but our database does record which account gave which rating. Supabase hosts that database for us. We’ll name the service that stores any site form before that form opens.

Ages

The Slicio Crust Club, the taste test page and the waitlist are for ages 13 and up. The taste test page asks you to check a box that says you are 13 or older. If you are under 13, please don’t send us anything. If we learn that we collected information from someone under 13, we’ll delete it.

Delete your account

  • In the app: Account, then Delete account.
  • On the web: sign in on the taste test page, then use Delete my account at the bottom of the page. The page asks you to confirm before it deletes anything.

Deleting removes your name, email, member code, crusts, votes, taste test ratings and comments, requests you sent from the app, and the sign-in record. It can’t be undone. It doesn’t always remove Slicio from the list of connected apps in your Apple or Google account. If you want it gone from that list, remove Slicio in your Apple or Google account settings too.

Contact

Questions, or something that doesn’t work? Email sliciopizza@gmail.com.
